Time and the body are central to fitness, yet the sociology of sport has rarely theorized fitness as a temporal practice. Drawing on carnal sociology and sociological theories of time, the study treats fitness as a temporal craft and identifies three moves through which young adults recalibrate the aim, recompose the rhythm, and repair rupture in their exercise routines across the transition to adulthood.
