This paper investigates how young adults aged 18-30 define and relate the concepts of fitness and health in their everyday lives. Drawing on ethnographic interviews, it examines how participants navigate competing cultural narratives about appearance, performance, self-care, and wellness, revealing the diverse meanings they attach to being both 'fit' and 'healthy.'
